This policy explains how Sprywave Technologies Limited collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit sprywave.com, contact us or subscribe to our updates. It is written to align with Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

1. Who We Are

Sprywave Technologies Limited (“Sprywave”, “we”, “us”) is a technology company based in Accra, Ghana. For personal data collected through this website and our sales and support enquiries, Sprywave is the data controller.

When our clients use Sprywave platforms such as SpryConnect, SpryDesk, GeoPulse Cloud or MISA ERP, the client organisation is usually the data controller for its own customers’, staff’s or students’ data, and Sprywave acts as a data processor on its documented instructions. Each platform has its own privacy notice, which applies alongside this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect what we need for the purposes described below:

  • Contact and enquiry details: name, email address, phone number, role or position, the service or product you are interested in, and your message, when you use our contact form, email or call us.
  • Newsletter details: your email address when you subscribe to updates.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit, recorded in our server and security logs.
  • Business relationship data: contact and billing details of client representatives when we provide services or products under a contract.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data (such as health, religious or financial account information) through this website. Please do not include it in messages to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals and arrange demos.
  • To provide, support and invoice the services and products you have contracted.
  • To send updates you have subscribed to. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in each email.
  • To keep our website secure, prevent abuse and diagnose technical problems.
  • To meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

In line with Act 843, we process personal data only where we have a lawful basis, namely:

  • Consent, for example when you subscribe to updates. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract, where processing is needed to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, or to perform one.
  • Legitimate interests, such as responding to business enquiries and securing our website, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation, where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

5. Sharing Your Information

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, security and content delivery (for example Cloudflare), and email providers, under confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, auditors and accountants, where necessary.
  • Regulators, law enforcement or courts, where we are legally required to do so.

6. International Transfers

Some of our service providers, including content delivery and font services, may process data on servers outside Ghana. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data receives a level of protection consistent with Act 843, for example through contractual safeguards.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contract: up to 24 months from our last contact.
  • Client and contract records: for the duration of the relationship and as long as tax and accounting laws require afterwards.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe.
  • Server and security logs: up to 90 days, unless needed to investigate an incident.

When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

8. How We Protect Your Information

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls limited to staff who need the information, regular updates to our systems, and confidentiality obligations for our team and providers. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected people and the Data Protection Commission of a breach where required by law.

9. Your Rights Under Act 843

You have the right to:

  • Ask whether we hold personal data about you and request a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete or stop using your information, where there is no overriding reason to keep it.
  • Object to processing for direct marketing, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@sprywave.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. If your data is held in a Sprywave platform on behalf of an organisation (for example your school or employer), please contact that organisation first; we will support them in responding.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website uses only the cookies and similar technologies needed to deliver and secure the site, such as security cookies set by our content delivery provider. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

11. Children

This website is intended for businesses and organisations, not for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this website. Student data processed in MISA ERP is handled on behalf of the school, as described in that platform’s privacy notice.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Where changes are significant, we will highlight them on this website.
