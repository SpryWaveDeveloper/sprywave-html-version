Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ensure the quality of work from virtual teams?

We implement robust QA processes, regular code reviews, and performance tracking to ensure high-quality deliverables at every stage of development.

What collaboration tools do you use for remote teams?

Our teams work in the tools you already use — typically Slack or Microsoft Teams for chat, Jira or Trello for task tracking, GitHub or GitLab for code, and Google Meet or Zoom for stand-ups.

How do you protect sensitive information and ensure data security?

Every team member signs an NDA, access is role-based and revoked when work ends, and we handle personal data in line with Ghana’s Data Protection Act (Act 843).

What’s the typical timeframe to set up a virtual development team?

Most teams are ready to start within 1–3 weeks, depending on the roles and seniority you need.

Can the virtual team work in my time zone?

Yes. We schedule working hours to give you a solid daily overlap, and can arrange fully aligned hours where your project needs it.

Do you provide post-launch support and maintenance?

Yes. We offer ongoing support and maintenance plans covering bug fixes, updates, monitoring and small enhancements after launch.

What happens if I need to scale my team quickly?

Tell us what you need and we’ll add developers from our vetted pool, usually within a few weeks. Scaling down is just as simple.

Can I directly manage the virtual development team?

Absolutely. You can manage the team day to day yourself, or have a Sprywave project manager coordinate the work and report to you.

Do you offer specialized teams for specific technologies or industries?

Yes. We put together teams around your stack — such as Laravel, React, Python or AWS — and your industry, including fintech, retail and healthcare.

What is the payment structure for outsourcing services?

Teams are billed monthly per team member, with no long-term lock-in. Fixed-price options are available for well-defined projects.
